## 🟢 Incluido en Anki
- **Grammar Point**: 刚 / 刚才
  - **Phrase**: 我刚开始准备考试，虽然考试看起来还很远，但时间过得很快。
  - **Translation**: I have just started preparing for the exam; although it seems far away, time passes quickly.
